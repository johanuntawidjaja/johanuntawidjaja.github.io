September 2026 · Building with AI

A prototype needs a test loop

Two masterclasses connected career direction with a practical opportunity to build: Jurnal Lentera Bursa.

Direction, then execution

“Designing Your Future: Strategic Life & Career Planning in the Age of AI”, led by Andrew Tan, was followed by S. Anand’s “How to Build AI Products – and Prove They Work”. I applied the latter to a problem I had seen at home: my mother’s handwritten stock records.

AI as my teammate

AI helped me move from requirements to a working application. The important work around that speed was checking what came back: calculations, expected behaviour, access verification, and what data the application was allowed to transmit.

An early body of evidence

The current record is 52 automated checks and four authenticated testers across 17 monitored sessions. That gives me specific evidence to discuss and a bounded result to improve, rather than a reason to declare the product finished.
