The engineering loop

  1. 01

    Requirement

    Design a doorbell intended to operate without mains power.

  2. 02

    Sizing

    Use a low-sun energy budget rather than average sunlight.

  3. 03

    Revision

    Add openings after the enclosure muffled sound and trapped heat.

  4. 04

    Next test

    Measure temperature, sound level and battery state over low-sun days.

Inspect contribution and limits+

Calculated

The photovoltaic and battery sizing supported an off-mains design target under the assumed low-sun conditions.

Built and observed

The prototype was assembled and bench-tested. Enclosure problems prompted a revision; long-term outdoor autonomy was not established.

These artefacts document the prototype and its revision. No field-test duration, acoustic improvement or thermal reduction is claimed without measurements.

01

Bench checks are not a field trial.

I bench-tested the electronics and charge path. The next useful evidence would be battery state through low-sun days, enclosure temperature and sound level at the listener. Those measurements would test the assumptions behind the design.
