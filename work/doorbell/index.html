Physical product

A doorbell, off the mains

A solar-powered doorbell designed around its energy budget, then revised around the realities of sound and heat.

My role

Energy sizing · CAD · Electronics

Tools & approach

Fusion 360 · Battery & solar sizing · Fabrication

When / Status

2026
Course prototype

01

Start with the energy budget.

For Designing Energy Systems, I sized the photovoltaic panel and 18650 battery for a low-sun period. The calculations supported operation without mains power. That was a design target, rather than a claim of proven long-term autonomy.

02

The enclosure was part of the circuit.

I produced the Fusion 360 CAD set for the button, receiver, solar-panel housing, hinges, and assembly. The first sealed design muffled the speaker and contributed to heat inside the casing. I revised the openings to improve airflow and let sound travel.

03

What I would validate next.

The next useful evidence would be a longer outdoor test: battery state through low-sun days, temperature inside the enclosure, and sound level at the listener. The prototype connected the sizing calculation to a physical object; sustained field performance remains a separate test.
