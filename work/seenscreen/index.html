My work

Station, train and display CAD; final product design; posters and presentation slides.

Team context

The overall concept and proposed sensing pipeline were team work. The benches were modelled by a teammate.

The visual artefacts establish the design and my contribution. They do not demonstrate a deployed system, measured crowd reduction or validated passenger behaviour.

01

Explain the system, not just the object.

The final presentation connects the passenger problem, proposed sensing pipeline and display. I produced the presentation slides and posters so the team’s concept could be understood as a system, rather than a standalone CAD model.

02

The next design question.

A more complete evaluation would test whether passengers can understand the display quickly, where they notice it, and how the information changes their boarding decision. The CAD communicates the setting; it does not establish usability on its own.
