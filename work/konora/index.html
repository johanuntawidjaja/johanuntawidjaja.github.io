My role

Tools & approach

When / Status

May 2026
Team business concept

01

Connection, with context.

Our team explored how performers and clients find each other through word of mouth and personal contacts. Konora proposed an easier way to arrange events while protecting performers from exploitation. Earning local trust was part of the proposal’s starting point.

02

The part I presented.

I presented the Business Model Canvas and the closing portion of the pitch. Working between English and Indonesian also meant helping the team communicate assumptions that did not translate automatically. The result was a business concept, not a launched marketplace.
