All experiences/Making motion more reliable

SOAR / SCRT · VEX U

Making motion more reliable

My work on SUTD’s VEX U team has moved from protective casing and motor assembly to autonomous routines and camera-calibration tools.

My role

Hardware, then software from April 2026

Tools & approach

C++ · PROS · Python · OpenCV

When / Status

October 2025–present
Ongoing team work

01

Begin at the bench.

I joined the hardware team after SOAR Challenge. I helped create protective casing for the robot brain, assemble motors, and work on sheet-metal structures. That gave me a physical starting point before moving into software.

02

Test the movement on the robot.

I programmed controller movements and an autonomous routine in C++ using PROS. Small calibration errors repeatedly made the pin drop miss its target, so I adjusted and reran the sequence. We had not achieved well-tuned PID control; in retrospect, those foundations needed more attention before the fine movements.

03

Recover from a bad camera input.

My later Python/OpenCV contributions added camera reconnection, rejection of missing or corrupted frames, exposure controls, and calibration-file handling across multiple robot configurations. This was a contribution to the team’s existing tool, not ownership of the complete vision system.

The team received the Laurel Award at the SUTD Student Achievement Awards 2026.
